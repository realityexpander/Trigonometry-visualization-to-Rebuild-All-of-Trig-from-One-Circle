Function visibility

Any combination
Three physical actions × two axes = six functions. “Shadow” uses coordinate projections, “Touch” measures along the tangent lines x = ±1 or y = ±1, and “Cut” measures the radial segments to those lines. Outside quadrant I, construction lines move to the tangent on the matching side of the circle, with signed values displayed above.

Show component projections
Rotate a length of cos B by A. Its horizontal and vertical projections are cos A cos B and sin A cos B. The perpendicular piece of length sin B contributes +cos A sin B vertically, but −sin A sin B horizontally.
